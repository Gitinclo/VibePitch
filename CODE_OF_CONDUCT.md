# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`)

**Version:** 2.1 (Adapted for VibePitch Studio)

## 1. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **VibePitch Studio** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy engineering community.

## 2. Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy, candor, and constructive technical feedback during code reviews.
- Respecting differing perspectives, business verticals, and regional markets.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on architecture, security rules, and test coverage.
- Focusing on what is best for the security, privacy, and reliability of the community and end users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, or committing real API keys/secrets without explicit permission.
- Intentionally introducing backdoors, malicious dependencies, or weakened security rules (`allow read, write: if true;`).

## 3. Enforcement Responsibilities

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (source code, issues, pull requests, discussions, and documentation) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## 5. Reporting & Enforcement Guidelines

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `conduct@vibepitch.studio`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly:
1. **Correction**: A private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation.
2. **Warning**: A formal warning with consequences for continued behavior, including temporary restriction from pull request or issue interaction.
3. **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from repository participation for severe or repeated violations.
